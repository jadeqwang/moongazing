# 望明月 Moongazing: Visual Research

*Compiled 2026-10-06 for the music video. Audience: SF tech Twitter and YouTube. Inputs reviewed: lyrics and notes, `inputs/jade_watercolor.png`, `inputs/jade_in_outfit.png`, `inputs/jade_committee.png`.*

## 0. TL;DR

1. **The central visual pun is in the lyric already.** 举头望明月，低头思故乡 means "raise my head, see the bright moon; lower my head, think of home." On a lunar south-pole base, raising your head shows you *Earth*, so the poem flips. Build the trap breakdown around this one move: head up on 举头, head down on 低头. That is the MV's "point" choreography and its thumbnail.
2. **Structure the MV as a handscroll (手卷) cut into chapters (Hero-style color blocks).** The scroll gives one continuous geography from West Lake to the Moon. The color chapters give the K-pop reset at each genre change.
3. **Lyrics should appear as inscriptions (题跋) with red seals (印章).** Each seal stamp lands on a downbeat. Burned-in bilingual lyrics are normal in C-pop (KTV) and help English speakers with the Chinese lines.
4. **2026 space imagery is unusually rich:** Artemis II's *Earthset* and *Hello, World* (April 2026), Starship reaching orbit (Sept 28), NASA's "Moon Base… this time to stay" program at the south pole, Chang'e-6's far-side samples, and Long March 10/Mengzhou tests. Quote compositions, not logos or flags.
5. **The biggest risk is reading as AI slop.** "Slop" was Merriam-Webster's 2025 Word of the Year, BLACKPINK's "JUMP" was hit with AI accusations, and the song itself is Suno-assisted. The visuals have to show a human hand: real ink, real tea, real fabric, details that hold up to scrutiny.

**Recommended direction:** **A + C hybrid, "The Scroll with Chapters"** (section 7).

### Notes from the existing assets
- **Character sheet** (`jade_in_outfit.png`): white cropped jacket with orange stripes, a **"1420 MHz"** patch (the hydrogen line, the "universal unit" used on the Pioneer plaque and Voyager record), **"RARE EARTH"** with a **pale blue dot** on the back, orange headphones, navy cargo pants with orange straps, and a **Yagi antenna** (amateur radio, which suggests Earth-Moon-Earth "moonbounce"). That is already a strong space-age palette. Keep it.
- **Watercolor portrait** (`jade_watercolor.png`): ink-and-wash on rice paper, plum blossom, two red seals, a jade pendant. This is the Earth/West Lake register.

**Six concrete Zhang techniques to steal**
1. **One hue per section**, set by costume + set + practical light + grade, never by grade alone.
2. **Fabric in wind as an emotional meter.** Still sleeves in Verse 1, then billowing silk on the Hook. Chang'e's traditional 披帛 (silk shawl ribbon) can become a tether line or parachute cords later.
3. **Symmetrical mass framing.** Rows of the clones, mission-control desks, launch-pad lights. Hold the center.
4. **Scroll reveals.** A lateral camera move uncovers new territory at constant speed.
5. **Nature as particle system.** Leaves, snow, rain, arrows. Swap in regolith dust and ice crystals for the Moon.
6. **A single saturated accent** against monochrome (Shadow's blood becomes our seal red, or Earth blue).

### 2.1 Lyrics as inscriptions with seal stamps: a system

**Layout rules**
- **Chinese lines**: **vertical**, top to bottom, columns read right to left, placed in the 留白 (empty sky or water) of the frame, never over a face. Use a real brush-calligraphy font (行书 or 行楷 such as 汉仪 / 方正 families), or better, **hand-brushed by a calligrapher and scanned**. Hand-brushed type is the strongest anti-slop signal available.
- **English lines**: horizontal, small, **set like a colophon** in a quiet serif such as Cormorant, EB Garamond or Source Serif, at 40–50% of the Chinese size, in ink-gray rather than black. Use pinyin only for 举头望明月 and 举杯邀明月, where the audience may want to sing along.
- **Reveal**: characters **write on by stroke order** (brush wipe). Each column takes one bar (1.71 s at 140 BPM). Do not use a typewriter effect or a fade.
- **Seal (印章)**: **one red seal stamps on the downbeat ending each lyric line**, with a 2-frame scale overshoot and a subtle paper-dent shadow. Alternate **朱文 (red characters)** and **白文 (white characters on red)**.
- **Seal content** (carve real ones or have them carved; ~US$30–80 on Taobao or from SF Chinatown seal carvers): `望明月` (title), `明月` (moon), `思故乡` (homesick), the artist name, `1420` (the hydrogen-line nerd seal), `广寒` (Chang'e's palace "Vast Cold", which doubles as the name of the lunar base, since the south pole's shadowed craters are among the coldest places in the solar system).
- **Escalation**: Verse 1 gets one seal per stanza; the Hook one per line; the Drop seals land **on every beat**, filling the frame Qianlong-style; the Outro has **one** last seal.
- **Colophon credits (题跋)**: end the MV with a colophon scroll that records provenance the way collectors did. *English poem "To Li Bai", Oyez Review, 2007 · melody from a 2011 jam with Charlie van Norman · Chinese lines by Li Bai (701–762) · song 2026.* This is honest about how the song was made, gives the AI-assisted parts a human lineage, and is moving.
- **Optional extra inscriptions** (not sung, for the artist to use as background 题跋):
  - 李商隐《嫦娥》"嫦娥应悔偷灵药，碧海青天夜夜心" ("Chang'e must regret stealing the elixir: blue sea, blue sky, her heart night after night"). This is the canonical homesick-Chang'e couplet.
  - 李白《把酒问月》"今人不见古时月，今月曾经照古人" ("People now never saw the ancient moon, but this moon once shone on the ancients"). This bridges Li Bai and the astronaut.
  - 苏轼 "但愿人长久，千里共婵娟" ("may we share the moon though a thousand li apart"). It literalizes **moonbounce radio** (her Yagi antenna).

### 4.2 Cringe and slop flags

- 🚩 **AI-generated hero imagery**: morphing transitions, waxy faces, "girl in hanfu on the moon" Midjourney compositions, glowing particle swirls. After the Ghibli-filter backlash (Mar 2025), M-W's "slop" (Dec 2025) and the BLACKPINK "JUMP" AI accusations (Jul 2025), **this audience pattern-matches AI on sight and punishes it**. If generative tools are used, use them for plates and roto, never for faces or hands, and show the hand-made layer on top (scanned ink, real seals).
- 🚩 **Fake-looking NASA details.** The five-sided-window fake was caught in days. Window shape, suit details and Earth phase versus lunar geometry will be checked.
- 🚩 **Propaganda pastiche** (Soviet or PRC), **flags, and US-vs-China framing.** Both the US and China are racing to the same Shackleton rim, and an SF audience will read a hero flag as a statement. Keep the base **fictional and personal** (广寒, a patch with her name and a rabbit) and keep national flags out of hero shots. "Soviet brainrot" also makes that poster style read as an AI meme in 2026.
- 🚩 **Musk/X iconography, "e/acc", "accelerate", "we're so back" text, line-go-up charts, Wojak.** These are polarizing or dated. Quote the **hardware** (chopsticks), not the personalities or slogans.
- 🚩 **Orientalist clichés**: the "chop suey"/wonton font, gongs on every hit, red lanterns plus dragons everywhere, Fu Manchu mustaches, cherry blossoms (those are Japanese; use **plum blossom 梅** for winter, as in the watercolor already).
- 🚩 **Over-cutting the guzheng intro.** K-pop pacing in a 留白 section wastes the contrast you need for the Drop.

## 5. K-pop MV attention mechanics, as concrete rules

**Tempo math at 140 BPM**: 1 beat = 0.429 s (≈10.3 frames at 24 fps); 1 bar = 1.714 s (≈41 frames); 8 bars = 13.7 s. The trap half-time Breakdown *feels* like 70 BPM: snare on beat 3, one cut per bar.

1. **The first 3 seconds are a promise, not an establishing shot.** Open on the most arresting image *and* the face. Our cold open: a **red seal slams `望明月` onto rice paper on the first guzheng pluck**, then a hard cut to her eyes reflecting a crescent (Earth? Moon? keep it ambiguous). Put a title card within 2 s; it also helps thumbnails and search. *Refs:* NewJeans **"OMG"** opens on a non-sequitur line ("I was actually an iPhone") in a psych ward, so curiosity comes before music ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OMG_(NewJeans_song))). BLACKPINK and aespa open on full-glam close-ups.
2. **Cut grid by section.** Intro/V1: one cut every 2–4 bars, or long scroll takes. V2: every bar. Hook: every 2 beats, with **one cut exactly on the vocal downbeat** of each line. Breakdown: every bar on the snare, with **hi-hat rolls → stutter/speed-ramp**. Build: halve cut length every 2 bars (1 bar → 2 beats → 1 beat → ½ beat) to mirror the snare roll. Drop: **white or color flash frame on the 1**, then cuts every beat for 8 bars, then **one long hold** so it can breathe. *(Industry shorthand: "cutting runs on the eights: full-body formation wide, point-move close-up on the hook, whip to the next line".)*
3. **Center-framed, symmetrical hook.** "Symmetric framing on the hook drop is non-negotiable." Her in center, clones flanking (对影成三人 = **three in frame** for the 对影 line: her, her shadow, the moon).
4. **A "point" move fans can copy.** Two candidates, both from the lyrics:
   - **举杯 cup-raise** (raise tea or wine cup to the moon, then tilt) on **举杯邀明月** in the Hook.
   - **举头 / 低头 head-up / head-down** on the Breakdown line, with the camera inverting to show Earth.

   *Refs:* Super Junior "Sorry, Sorry" hand-rub, TWICE "TT" hand sign, PSY "Gangnam Style". These are simple, done with the hands or head, and work at selfie scale. **Shoot a 9:16 version of each point move for Shorts and Reels.**
5. **Color reset at each section.** Every genre change gets a new dominant hue and often a new set. aespa does this hard: "Supernova" is maximalist chrome and green, while "Whiplash" is two sets and a limited palette ([Envi Media](https://envimedia.co/aespa-establish-icon-status-in-whiplash-the-5th-mini-album/)). NewJeans "Ditto" uses a camcorder-texture shift as its "color" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ditto_(song))). For us, this is the Hero chapter system.
6. **Texture switch equals genre switch.** "Ditto" goes from 6mm camcorder to clean digital. Black Myth changes medium per chapter. Our sequence: ink on paper → mineral color → cinematic live action/3D → stele-rubbing negative → full-color space → back to paper.
7. **Traditional and trap crossover precedents** (the closest K-pop analogs to this song):
   - **Agust D "Daechwita" (2020)**: Joseon military-band 대취타 intro played by the National Gugak Center, switching to a **trap** beat; filmed on palace sets with modern intrusions ([Rolling Stone India](https://rollingstoneindia.com/bts-suga-returns-as-the-deadly-agust-d-with-daechwita)).
   - **BTS "IDOL" (2018)**: hanbok, the **moon rabbit**, tiger, and pansori shouts over trap/EDM ([Soompi](https://www.soompi.com/article/1219335wpp/watch-bts-brings-korean-tradition-world-stage-awe-inspiring-idol-mv)).
   - **KPop Demon Hunters / "Golden" (2025)**: minhwa tiger-and-magpie folk painting made pop.
   - **Lay Zhang "莲 Lit" (2020)**: the C-pop analog. Guofeng instruments plus trap plus future bass; an army charges the Qin palace; lotus motifs and Peking opera ([Rolling Stone India](https://rollingstoneindia.com/watch-lay-zhang-make-an-epic-comeback-with-lit)).
8. **中国风 MV grammar (Jay Chou / Vincent Fang).**
   - **青花瓷 (2008)** uses a **dual timeline**: a past-life romance, then a cut through time to a **modern auction house** where the same porcelain reunites the lovers ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_and_White_Porcelain)). That past-to-present object bridge is our structure. Make **the tea cup** (snow-brewed tea, 举杯) the object that crosses from West Lake to the Moon.
   - **东风破 (2003)** is a Chinese melody in an R&B arrangement.
   - Classic C-pop MVs burn in **KTV-style lyrics**, so on-screen lyrics feel native for Chinese viewers.
9. **Kinetic typography references.**
   - **Saul Bass**: *Vertigo* (1958) spirals and *Anatomy of a Murder* (1959) cut-paper type. Graphic shapes cut to rhythm.
   - **Kyle Cooper**: *Se7en* (1995). Hand-scratched, jittery, physical type. The right register for the trap breakdown.
   - **Prince, "Sign o' the Times" (1987, dir. Bill Konersman)**: widely called the **first lyric video**; all-type, color pulses with the beat ([SIGGRAPH](https://history.siggraph.org/animation-video-pod/sign-of-the-times-mix-efex-pacific-video-by-konnersman/)).

   - **Billie Eilish lyric videos**: minimal all-caps sans, lots of black space. That is 留白 in Western form.

## 7. Three candidate art directions

Song map used below: **Intro** (guzheng/erhu) · **V1** (sparse) · **V2** (orchestral rock + taiko) · **Hook** · **Breakdown** (trap, 举头望明月) · **Build** · **Drop** (trance + erhu) · **Outro** (guzheng).
Story spine: West Lake in winter → teahouse with snow-brewed tea → launch → ascent → south-pole base 广寒 → she looks *up* and sees Earth → euphoric "invite the moon" toast to Earth → alone again with tea.
**Through-line object: the tea cup.** In V1 it reflects the moon on West Lake. In the Drop it reflects Earth.

### A. 手卷 "The Handscroll": ink to mineral to space

- **Concept:** the whole MV is **one continuous right-to-left handscroll**. The camera never leaves the paper; the paper becomes the world. At liftoff the scroll **burns and splits** like *Fuchun*: the left half (Earth) and right half (Moon) separate.
- **Palette:** rice paper `#EFE6D2` · ink `#1C1B19` · ink wash gray `#7D7A73` · **azurite** `#2F5D8C` · **malachite** `#3E8E6E` · ochre `#B98A4E` · **cinnabar seal** `#C0392B` · moon-paper (inverted) `#121417` · earthshine `#9DB7D5`
- **Texture:** scanned mulberry and xuan paper grain, real ink bleeds (shot practically and composited), dry-brush 皴 texture strokes on mountains *and* craters, gold-leaf flecks for stars. Hardware drawn in **工笔** linework over the wash.
- **Typography:** hand-brushed vertical 行书 lyrics plus small serif English colophons, with **seals on downbeats** (2.1). Countdowns use Chinese numerals.
- **Across the genre shifts:**
  - Intro/V1: monochrome ink, 70% 留白, West Lake snow (断桥残雪), willows as single strokes.
  - V2: **mineral blue-green floods in** (Wang Ximeng) with the strings. Taiko = 泼墨 splashes. The teahouse is drawn like the *Qingming scroll*.
  - Hook: the rocket is a gongbi drawing; the exhaust is splashed ink.
  - Breakdown: **paper inverts to stele-rubbing black**, white characters, a single Earth in azurite at the top of frame (举头).
  - Build: the scroll scrolls faster and seals stamp faster.
  - Drop: full mineral color, Earthset composition, Huaisu cursive flying, seals on every beat.
  - Outro: back to blank paper, one seal, colophon credits.
- **Risks:** it can feel museum-polite or slow for K-pop pacing (fix this with hard cuts *within* the scroll and match-cuts between scroll planes). It needs a skilled 2.5D compositor. It also overlaps with the 2008 Olympics, so avoid any crowd-on-LED-floor look.

### B. "Mission Patch × Nianhua": 1976 standards manual meets 1984 moon-palace poster

- **Concept:** flat **graphic-design** world. NASA 1975 grid minimalism and the warmth of Chinese 1980s nianhua space posters (月宫小客人) meet her existing white/orange/navy outfit. The look is screen-printed: mission patches, decals, signage.
- **Palette:** off-white `#F2EFE8` · safety orange `#F26B21` · NASA red (PMS 179, ≈`#E03C31`) · navy `#1B2A4A` · pale-blue-dot `#8FB8DE` · nianhua pink `#E98A9A` · mustard `#E8B23A` · regolith gray `#9A9893`
- **Texture:** risograph or screen-print misregistration, halftone dots, flat cel shading, sticker die-cut edges.
- **Typography:** worm-era geometric sans (Helvetica Now / Neue Haas, or Inter Tight wide-tracked caps) plus **思源宋体 Source Han Serif** for Chinese. Lyrics as **mission-control lower thirds** and **countdown clocks**; Chinese lines on stencils. Seals still appear, as rubber stamps on checklists.
- **Across the genre shifts:**
  - Intro/V1: a **travel-poster West Lake** (flat shapes, limited inks).
  - V2: a patch-design montage with the committee at consoles.
  - Hook: a poster-diagonal launch.
  - Breakdown: blueprint/negative.
  - Drop: Bonestell/McCall painterly blow-out (the only painted section).
  - Outro: back to a poster.
- **Risks:** **retro-futurism is overused** (*For All Mankind*, Wes Anderson, every agency deck). Poster pastiche now reads as **"Soviet brainrot" AI slop**, and nianhua/propaganda carries political baggage. Flat graphics dampen the homesick emotion and fight the guzheng sections. **Use this as a seasoning, not the meal.**

### C. "色块分幕 Chromatic Chapters": Zhang Yimou color acts, cinematic live action or 3D

- **Concept:** each section is a **Hero-style color chapter**, carried by costume, set, practical light and grade together. Real fabric, real snow, real water. One practical trick per act: submerged West Lake deck, underwater shoot for low-g, a wind machine for 披帛 silk.
- **Palette by act:**
  - Intro/V1 **Snow & Ink**: `#F4F4F1` / `#5B5E62` (Shadow).
  - V2 **Jade Bamboo**: `#2E7D5B` / `#A8C686` (HOFD).
  - Hook **Cinnabar**: `#B3221B` / `#E8573F` (Hero red, launch fire).
  - Breakdown **Moon Blue**: `#0F1E33` / `#1E3A5F` / `#8FA9C9` (Hero blue × Chinese Ghost Story).
  - Build **Strobe White**.
  - Drop **Corona Gold → Earth Blue**: `#E9C46A` → `#3D7CC9` (Artemis II eclipse, then Earthset).
  - Outro: return to **Snow** `#F4F4F1` with one red seal `#C0392B`.
- **Texture:** filmic grain, atmospheric haze, particles (snow → ash → regolith dust → ice), silk.
- **Typography:** large, sparse calligraphy **projected onto surfaces** (snow, silk, the lunar wall) in the style of Cloud Gate *Cursive*. The English is subtitle-quiet.
- **Across the genre shifts:** hard **color resets on section downbeats**, K-pop-style. Symmetric clone formations for the Hook and Drop. A Falcon-Heavy-style mirrored descent. The 举头/低头 move in Moon Blue with Earth overhead.
- **Risks:** **budget**, and the temptation to fake it with AI or a LUT, which is the fastest route to slop. Color blocking without art-department commitment looks like a filter. Zhang Yimou homage is well-trodden in Chinese media, so it needs the space twist to feel new.

### Recommendation: **A + C, "The Scroll with Chapters"** (B only as accent)

**Use A as the backbone**: the handscroll world, ink-to-mineral color, inscriptions plus seals as the lyric system, the burned-and-split *Fuchun* scroll as the homesickness device. **Use C's per-section color reset** inside the scroll so each genre change gets a K-pop-grade visual reset. **Use B only for hardware and UI** (her patch, base signage 广寒, mission clocks), never as a whole-frame style.

This is the most defensible choice for three reasons:
1. It is **visibly hand-made**: real ink, real seals, a calligrapher's brush. That is the only credible answer to "is this AI slop?" for a Suno-assisted track in front of SF Twitter.
2. It turns the song's **genre whiplash into a medium change** (Black Myth's per-chapter styles, "Ditto"'s texture shift), so the jumps read as design, not chaos.
3. It **earns the space-nerd payoffs**: Earthset through a four-sided window, the chopsticks catch, the pale blue dot plus a seal as the "you are here" marker. They land because the first half was so quiet.

**One-line pitch:** *A Song-dynasty handscroll that keeps unrolling past West Lake, past the launch tower, onto the Moon, where 举头望明月 finally means looking up at Earth.*
