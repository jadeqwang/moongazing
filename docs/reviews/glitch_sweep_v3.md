# Visual glitch sweep — fullcut v3

Completed 7 October 2026. Scope: `render/out/fullcut_v3_540p.mp4`, 960×540, 24 fps, 5,305 decoded frames, 221.042 s. All **114/114 shots** are accounted for: 74 shots with findings and 40 clean shots. The table has 108 finding rows (13 P0, 80 P1, 15 P2); multiple rows can describe one shot.

This completes the [working ledger](glitch_sweep_v3/observations.md), retaining prior reviews and adding only the remaining 8.3/8.4/9.1 reviews and flagged close looks. Existing frames, sheets and numeric results were reused. No extraction, contact-sheet regeneration, numeric recomputation, render, or edits under `render/src` or `media/` were performed.

Severity: **P0 = anyone would notice; P1 = noticeable on a second watch; P2 = minor.** **KNOWN** means the defect is specifically described in the [director’s Oct 7 notes](../REVISION_NOTES_OCT7.md), including mapped clusters; **NEW** means this particular defect/manifestation was not named there. A global rule such as no frozen people or no red uniforms does not make every newly located violation KNOWN. The uniform stripe reference conflicts with the director’s newer no-red rule; affected rows disclose that conflict rather than treating the older stripe as permission.

Times are song seconds from the decoded v3 MP4. `fN` is a **zero-based frame index at 24 fps**, matching the saved frame filenames; time ranges below use the first and last affected reviewed frame, inclusive. Display clocks round to 0.1 s; exact frame times remain in song seconds. Whole-shot ranges include the saved first/last frames, not the next shot’s cut frame.

Probable causes distinguish **snapshot wiring** in [shots.json](glitch_sweep_v3/shots.json) from the current source, which has already changed in some places (notably 2.3, 7.C4/7.C5 and 7.D3/7.D3b). A source change is not evidence of a fix in this MP4. Roto-versus-take attributions are hypotheses unless wiring directly establishes the cause; no source-take review was repeated. Deliberate ink bleeds, card flashes, paper tears, graphic reveals, memory dissolves and appropriate credit/diagram holds are not findings.

## Systemic causes

These are engine/pipeline groups, not requests to patch every shot independently. Engine references: [roto/index.js](../../render/src/roto/index.js) and [roto/shader.js](../../render/src/roto/shader.js), read only for diagnosis. The shader multiplies the motion mask by the subject/difference mask, forces face regions through redraw unless a tracked painted-region carry wins, and combines that with `uLock`. Those mechanisms explain the recurring patterns, but cannot by themselves prove whether a particular source take also lacks movement.

- **Whole subjects retained outside the moving region.** Active water, dust, steam, one hand or one person moves while other people/body parts remain painted and held. Check full-body mattes and each take’s secondary movement before changing thresholds. Affected shots: **0.4, 2.1, 2.3, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4a, 3.4c, 4.2, 4.3, 4.5, 4.7, 5.1, 7.B1, 7.B1b, 7.B2, 7.B3, 7.C2, 7.C3, 7.C4a, 7.C4b, 7.C4c, 7.C4d, 7.C4e, 7.C4f, 7.C4g, 7.C4h, 7.C6, 7.D1, 7.D2, 7.D3d, 7.E1, 7.E1b, 7.E1c, 8.2b, 8.3**. Foreground astronaut in 5.1, Kenton/T in 7.C2 and the third-hand carry risk in 7.C3 are especially consequential.
- **Double contours during the `lock` dissolve.** Painted opening pose and moving redraw occupy different positions. Remove the opening hold/blend (`lock: 0`), enter at continuous motion and validate the cut pose/matte rather than merely shortening the dissolve. Affected shots: **0.6, 1.4, 1.6, 2.4, 3.1, 3.7b, 5.1, 7.B2, 7.B3, 7.C4a, 7.C4b, 7.C4c, 7.C4d, 7.C4e, 7.C4f, 7.C4g, 7.C4h, 7.D3c, 7.D3d**. Snapshot lock values, rather than newer section edits, explain the v3 handoffs.
- **Faces redrawn instead of carried from the painting.** Facial-line flattening and guide thresholds change eyes, nose, glasses, lip shape and likeness; missing angle-specific guides leave the 8.3 profile blank. Carry a tracked painted face when it can remain anatomically correct, or use safer angles/visors and reject takes with bad faces. Affected shots: **1.4, 2.3, 3.1, 3.4b, 3.6e, 3.7, 3.7b, 4.3, 7.B1, 7.B3, 8.3**.
- **Still plates substitute for moving people/actions.** Camera motion, steam, lettering or a JS signal does not supply body motion. Snapshot `plate`/assignment wiring establishes the still-layer cases; video assets already exist for several (2.5/3.1 family, 4.3b pool continuation, 7.D1 stage and J_7.D2 reach), while other replacements need validated takes. Make the scene-layer audit automatic and keep intended graphic stills exempt. Affected shots: **2.5, 3.5, 3.8, 4.3c, 5.3a, 5.3b, 5.3c, 5.3d, 5.3e, 5.3f, 5.3g, 5.3h, 5.4, 6.1, 6.2a, 6.2b, 6.3, 7.B4, 7.B5, 7.C5a, 7.C5b, 7.C5c, 7.C5d, 7.C1, 7.D3, 7.D3b, 7.D4, 8.2, 8.2b**. 5.3a–h are low-severity short cards; 8.2 is a major reunion failure.
- **Unphysical action or incomplete limb/tool movement.** Grips, strikes, hops, turns and bows must be physically coherent in the video before roto, then retain all coupled parts through redraw. Fix motion validation and object/limb continuity, not decorative particles alone. Affected shots: **3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 4.3c, 5.1, 7.B1b, 7.B2, 7.B5, 7.C3, 7.C5d, 7.D4, 7.E1, 7.E1b, 7.E1c, 8.2**.

In **8.2b the explicit opaque plate switch at 197.95 s** causes the first reset frame at **197.958 s/f4751**. Remove the hard return to a different pose and replace/reframe the smeared impact action. Fix references/composited decals centrally before new keyframes/takes; Chen/Lúcia’s older stripe colours conflict with the newer director rule. Confirmed uniform-chest findings are limited to exposed chests, not every occluded portrait. Affected shots: **1.2, 3.4c, 3.8, 4.7, 5.3a, 5.3e, 5.3f, 7.B3, 7.C2, 7.C4a, 7.C4e, 7.C4f, 7.C5b, 7.C5d, 7.D3d, 7.E1, 7.E1b, 7.E1c**.

- **Cyan skin rims from roto edges.** Affected shots: **3.7, 3.7b**. Repair matte edge contamination without suppressing intended saturated blue cabin/Earth/suit colours; colour-event screens alone do not establish a fringe defect.
