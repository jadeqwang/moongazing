# 望明月 · Moongazing — Director's Treatment (draft v0)

## The one idea
**Chinese landscape painting already knows what Earth and the Moon look like.**

- The Moon, photographed, *is* an ink-wash painting: grey on grey, no color, the regolith a paper grain.
- Earth, from space, is *青绿* — the azurite blue and malachite green mineral pigments of Wang Ximeng's
  千里江山图 (1113). The pale blue dot is literally painted in the pigments of a Song-dynasty scroll.
- So the film's color script is the story: **color = home.** On Earth everything is mineral blue-green on
  warm silk. On the Moon everything is monochrome ink — except Earth, hanging low on the horizon, the only
  colored thing in the universe. Homesickness is rendered as color you can't touch.

## Three "papers" (the style system)
| Paper | Where | Look | Palette |
|---|---|---|---|
| **绢 Silk** | Earth, memory, family | 青绿山水 + 工笔 fine line on aged silk; washes of ink; vermilion seals | silk ochre #E9DCC0, azurite #2F6690, malachite #3A8A6E, ink #1B1B1F, vermilion #C8312B |
| **宣 Xuan / ink** | The Moon | 水墨 monochrome ink on white paper; regolith = ink granulation; hard black shadows; Earth = the only color | paper #F2EFE8, ink greys, Earth in azurite/malachite |
| **磁青泥金 Indigo & gold** | Night, launch, space, the drop | gold-ink line on indigo-dyed paper (the Ming sutra technique), lines that *glow* for the trance drop | indigo #16213E, gold #D4A84B, ember #E86A33 |

Bridge technique: **界画 jiehua** — the classical ruled-line architectural painting. Rockets, launch towers and the
moonbase are drawn as jiehua: engineering drawings that are also Chinese paintings.

## Names
- Moonbase: **广寒 Guanghan Station** (Chang'e's palace on the Moon, "the Palace of Vast Cold") — lunar south pole.
- Agency: one Earth-wide agency; logo = a square vermilion seal with a single pale blue dot inside.
- Closing inscription: 海上生明月，天涯共此时 — "The bright moon rises over the sea; at the ends of the earth we share
  this moment." (Zhang Jiuling) — everyone separated by distance is looking at the same moon.

## The science rhyme that makes 低头思故乡 land
From the lunar south pole, Earth never rises high — it sits near the horizon. So on the Moon, to look at home you
literally **lower your head**. 举头望明月 (on Earth, the kids look *up* at the Moon) / 低头思故乡 (on the Moon, she
looks *down* at Earth). Two shots, one cut, mirrored tilt. (pending verification from the research agent)

## 对影成三人 = the Copernican lesson
"Raising my cup I invite the moon; with my shadow we make three." → Mom holds the flashlight (Sun), son holds Earth,
daughter holds Moon; their shadows on the wall perform an eclipse. Three people, three bodies, a family as a solar system.

2. Verse 1 — West Lake in winter, silk, memory; childhood (dance, wushu, math team).

5. Breakdown — whisper: 举头望明月 (kids look up) / 低头思故乡 (she looks down at Earth).

7. Outro — guzheng; ink returns; homecoming at Mid-Autumn (团圆); closing inscription.

## Research corrections (v0.1)
- 低头思故乡 staging: from the south pole Earth bobs within ±7° of the horizon. She stands on the high rim of
  Shackleton crater so Earth sits *below* eye level → she genuinely lowers her head. Sun behind her, her long shadow
  points toward Earth; Earth reflected in her gold visor.
- Mid-Autumn mirror: the family sees a FULL moon → she sees a NEW (dark) Earth, glittering with city lights; she finds
  the lights of home. Their lit fractions always sum to one whole — a hidden 团圆. (Mid-Autumn 2026 = Sep 25.)
- 月缺 (waning moon) on Earth ⇔ waxing Earth from the Moon. Use phase-pairs as transitions.
- Suits: China's real lunar suit is 望宇 Wangyu — shares the 望 of 望明月. Our agency suit can nod to it.
- Moonbase reality: regolith-covered grey mounds, vertical solar arrays on tall masts, fission reactor ~1 km away
  with radiator fins, glassy sintered landing pad far off. Sun ≤1.5° elevation: ridges are white islands in black ink.
- Calls: ~2.6 s round-trip pause; drop out when Earth dips below the horizon.
- Motif: Jade's green jade pendant = a piece of Earth she carries. Li Bai: 小时不识月，呼作白玉盘.
